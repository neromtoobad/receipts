## X / Twitter

Building RECEIPTS for the @sibylcap hackathon: six AI pundits forecasting real
matches, with two rules. Every piece of information costs real money — tips are
bought from ten informants over x402 on @base, priced 0.003 to 0.045 USDC, and

forecast, so the only thing that survives is what it wrote to memory.

Nobody tells them which informants are worth paying for. They find out by buying,
being wrong, and remembering. Two days in: 640 calls, 486 resolved, 9.83 USDC

## Discord

**RECEIPTS — six pundits who pay for their own information**

Six AI agents forecasting real football and crypto markets. Same model, same
prompt, same budget — genuinely identical. Two rules make it interesting: every
tip costs real USDC over x402 on Base, from ten informants priced 0.003 to 0.045
with no advertised hit rate; and the process dies after every forecast, so the
only thing that carries over is what it wrote to Sibyl Memory. That second rule
is what makes it a memory test rather than a long-conversation test.

So each agent has to work out at its own expense which informants are worth their
price, in which domain. Two days in: 640 calls, 486 resolved, 9.83 USDC spent,
102 pairings trusted and 89 written off. The finding I did not expect is that in
crypto every informant measures at or below zero — so the correct move there is
to buy nothing, and an agent with per-domain memory gets there on its own while
one global number per source never can. Board:
https://neromtoobad.github.io/receipts/ · Code:
https://github.com/neromtoobad/receipts

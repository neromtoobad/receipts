## Post 1 — build log: the deletion test

> Six AI pundits forecast real football and crypto markets, and every piece of
> evidence they use is a real USDC payment over x402 on @base. No free data. An
> agent that buys everything goes broke; one that buys nothing forecasts the base
> rate. The only way out is learning which sources are worth paying for — and I
> kill the process after every single forecast, so nothing survives except what
> the agent wrote to @sibylcap Memory.
>
> Ran the deletion test over 1,000 held-out events. Same model, same prompt, same
> budget, same informants at the same prices. With memory: $5.28 and 0.55
> informants a call. With memory deleted: $53.00 and 4.50 a call, every purchase
> tagged "no basis to choose". Ten times the money for a forecast that scores the
> same — 0.5658 vs 0.5664 Brier, a tie, and I report it as one. In crypto it's
> 71x, because the agent learned there's no signal there and very nearly stopped
> buying at all.
